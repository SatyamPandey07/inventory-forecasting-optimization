# InventoryAI — Product Roadmap & Future Architecture

Future expansion roadmap for **InventoryAI** (`v1.1+` and beyond).

---

## 🗺️ Future Expansion Pillars

### 1. Enterprise Competitor Scraping via Bright Data (Q3 2026)
- Integration with Bright Data Web Unlocker API to scrape competitor real-time pricing and stockout indicators for dynamic pricing response.

### 2. Enterprise Single Sign-On (SAML 2.0 / OAuth2 / Okta) (Q4 2026)
- SAML 2.0 and OIDC integration allowing enterprise retail organizations to authenticate via Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace.

### 3. Neural Time-Series Forecasting (DeepAR & Temporal Fusion Transformers) (Q1 2027)
- Expansion beyond Facebook Prophet to support PyTorch DeepAR and Temporal Fusion Transformers (TFT) for complex multi-variant retail demand networks.

### 4. Multi-Echelon Supply Network Optimization (Q2 2027)
- Support for multi-warehouse routing, regional distribution center safety stock allocation, and cross-docking logistics optimization.
